class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__deck">AI agents for small teams</p><div class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__content"><div><div class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__body"><p>Demarly gives founders and small teams AI agents that research, draft, and run scheduled workflows. You manage them through a CEO agent, with department leads and specialist workers underneath. I built it end to end, product to infrastructure.</p><p>Each agent has its own model and monthly budget, with an automatic pause at the cap. You can also bring your own API key and pay me nothing for compute. I gave up the markup to make the cost of an agent something a founder can actually predict.</p></div><p class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__link"><a href="https://www.demarly.ai" rel="noopener">Live at demarly.ai</a></p></div><figure class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__figure"><img class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__image" src="/personalWebsite/images/demarly-agent-hierarchy.png" width="2191" height="1309" alt="Demarly agent hierarchy with a CEO above CMO and CFO leads, connected to specialist workers." loading="lazy" decoding="async"/><figcaption class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__caption">The CEO, department leads, and specialist workers in Demarly.<a href="/personalWebsite/images/demarly-agent-hierarchy.png">View full-size <!-- -->Demarly<!-- --> screenshot</a></figcaption></figure></div></article><article class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__entry"><h3 class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__name">EdgeBet</h3><p class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__deck">The math behind the price</p><div class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__content CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__portrait"><div><div class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__body"><p>An iOS app for people who bet across several sportsbooks and want the math instead of opinions. It compares prices across books, peer-to-peer exchanges, and a prediction market, accounting for margin and fees, and turns that into three things: arbitrage, positive EV, and bonus bet conversion. I built the mobile app and the backend.</p><p>EdgeBet sells tools, not picks. That means there is no pick record to point at, but it is also the only version of this product I would put my name on.</p></div><p class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__link"><a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/edgebet/id6759763418" rel="noopener">On the App Store</a></p></div><figure class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__figure"><img class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__image" src="/personalWebsite/images/edgebet-market-comparison.png" width="1179" height="2556" alt="EdgeBet&#x27;s Arbs screen showing an arbitrage opportunity between BetUS and BetOpenly on a baseball game." loading="lazy" decoding="async"/><figcaption class="CompanyEntry-module__wlfYbG__caption">EdgeBet&#x27;s Arbs screen, showing an arbitrage opportunity between BetUS and BetOpenly on a baseball game.<a href="/personalWebsite/images/edgebet-market-comparison.png">View full-size <!-- -->EdgeBet<!-- --> screenshot</a></figcaption></figure></div></article></div></div></section><section id="work" aria-labelledby="h-work" class="Section-module__27SNAG__reveal  "><div class="wrap"><h2 id="h-work">Selected work</h2><div class="Section-module__27SNAG__body"><p>The part I actually care about is usually below the interface.</p><div class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__entries"><article class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__entry"><h3>A ledger that can&#x27;t count twice</h3><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__source">Demarly</p><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__body">The API and a background worker debit the same prepaid wallet, while Stripe can report the same payment twice. I put the rules in the database: an append-only ledger, row locks, and unique constraints keep concurrent writes and duplicate events from counting twice. <a href="/personalWebsite/writing/never-bill-twice/">Never bill twice</a>.</p></article><article class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__entry"><h3>A fair-price engine for betting markets</h3><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__source">EdgeBet</p><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__body">The engine removes bookmaker margin, estimates fair probability from a weighted consensus of books and exchanges, and scores prices after each venue&#x27;s fees. It also distrusts its own outliers: unusually high player-prop edges get re-scored conservatively, and the lower number wins.</p></article><article class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__entry"><h3>Cloud commitments as an optimization problem</h3><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__source">Nucli8, my product, built, not launched</p><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__body">AWS commitments lock in spending against uncertain usage. Nucli8 forecasts conservatively, then uses dynamic programming to cover the target with the fewest real instances. Purchases require human approval, with spend ceilings and a kill switch. Purchasing is off by default.</p></article><article class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__entry"><h3>Production AI infrastructure</h3><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__source">Applied AI and software engineering at multiple large enterprises, on engineering teams</p><p class="WorkEntry-module__mlVn8a__body">Before the companies, I worked on AI systems inside other people&#x27;s production environments. Document pipelines with confidence scores and human review, distributed inference, and one redesign that moved semantic search onto CPU with selective model fallback and took the serving footprint from 32 H100s to 4. These were team-built systems and my contribution was a piece of each, not the whole thing.</p></article></div></div></div></section><section id="writing" aria-labelledby="h-writing" class="Section-module__27SNAG__reveal  "><div class="wrap"><h2 id="h-writing">Writing</h2><div class="Section-module__27SNAG__body"><p>I write about engineering, AI, probability, and what I learn building companies.</p><ul class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__list"><li class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__item"><h3 class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__itemTitle"><a href="/personalWebsite/writing/the-30x-bug/">The 30x bug</a></h3><p class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__meta"><span>Engineering</span></p><p class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__excerpt">A dimensional-analysis mistake inflated a billing number by about 30x. So I built the skepticism into the pipeline.</p></li><li class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__item"><h3 class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__itemTitle"><a href="/personalWebsite/writing/never-bill-twice/">Never bill twice</a></h3><p class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__meta"><span>AI</span></p><p class="WritingList-module__DzX96a__excerpt">Two different processes write to the same wallet, and Stripe can send the same payment notification twice.
