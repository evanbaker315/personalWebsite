### Ruling on the five implementation decisions

**1. Selected Work source line in `--ink-2` rather than brown: ACCEPT.**
I compared the two annotation lines at 3x (`shots/crop-brown.png` "Probability and market data" against `shots/crop-grey.png` "EdgeBet"). They are clearly distinguishable, and the grey reads as *quieter* than the brown, which is the correct hierarchy: a company deck classifies the company and completes its heading, so it should hold attention; a work source attributes the work and is genuinely skippable. The coordinator's reading of spec section 8 was right, and the rendered result is better than brown would have been.

One documentation amendment, which I own and which requires **no code change**: spec section 2's `--ink-2` role line should name the Selected Work source line, and section 8's rule is more accurately stated as **brown = classification and dating** (company deck, writing category, article meta, article list markers) and **`--ink-2` = non-classifying secondary text** (work source, footer line, article blockquote). Both lists stay exhaustive. No third annotation color may be introduced later without a spec update. I will fold this into `design-spec.md` if the coordinator wants it recorded there; I did not edit that file this round.

**2. Category in brown where the date would go: ACCEPT.**
Rendered at 1440 and 375. It is real metadata, not invented, it uses brown's existing classification role (the article meta row already sets the category in brown per spec 5.9), and it keeps the meta slot from being empty. Under "Draft anything, send nothing" the brown line is the two characters "AI", which is thin under a 28-character title, but it reads as a label rather than as an orphan. No change.

**3. Footer as a 24px flex gap instead of middle dots: ACCEPT.**
Measured: `Evan Baker` / `2026` / `Built with Claude Code` at 15px `--ink-2`, one line at every width including 375 (footer line box measured at 23px tall = single line, and the row measures roughly 304px inside a 335px column). Consistent with the article meta row, and it avoids the middle-dot metadata construction spec section 9 rejects as a template tell. No change.

**4. `.band + section` at 64px: ACCEPT.**
Measured at 1440: band bottom 794, `#building` h2 top 858, gap exactly 64px, with the band's own 96px bottom padding above that. The total distance from the caption baseline to the h2 is 160px against a 128px standard section gap, but 96px of it is black, so the color change reads as "still the band" rather than as gap. In `shots/home-1440-t00-y0.png` and at 200% zoom (`shots/z200-home-y430.png`) the h2 sits at a comfortable distance and the section does not read as dropped. This is one of the two overrides section 10 permits, used correctly.

**5. Band caption as a layout element: ACCEPT, with one dependency.**
76 characters at 15px `--band-ink-2`. Measured: one line from a 600px viewport upward, two lines from 375 to 599. It sits 32px under the diagram and left-aligns with the column like everything else. Inside the 90-character ceiling section 5.2 sets. The one layout problem is not the string: between 480 and 639 the caption renders 528px wide above a 400px-wide diagram, so the caption is wider than the thing it captions. That is part of finding design-05, and the fix I propose there caps the caption to the diagram's width. If W03 replaces the string, hold it to 90 characters or fewer and re-check the 375px wrap. **Whether the sentence is Evan's to say is the writer's call, not mine; I am ruling on it as a layout element only.**

## Ideas and alternatives

- **The thorough fix for design-05, deliberately not requested for v1.** The root cause is that any SVG sized `width: 100%` scales its text with its geometry, and the spec's requirement that the diagram align exactly with the column edges forces the SVG to be fluid. The complete fix is to split the drawing: keep an SVG for the rule and ticks with `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, a fixed CSS `height`, and `vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"` on every line (so hairlines stay exactly 1px at any scale), and render the four station words as an HTML flex row at a literal 15px. That gives full-column alignment, invariant type, and invariant strokes at every width, and it removes the duplicate-text juggling between the two SVG variants. It is a component rewrite and it is not worth doing before the site ships. **Backlog, not a requirement.**
- **Rejected: centering the vertical diagram in the column between 480 and 687.** It would fill the dead space on the right, but everything else on the site is left-aligned to one column edge, and a single centered element would read as a mistake rather than as a choice.
- **Rejected: shrinking the vertical diagram's labels to 15px to match the horizontal variant.** 17px is deliberate; the mobile diagram is read at arm's length and it is the only content in the band on a phone. The goal is a stable range of 15-17px, not one number.
- **Considered and not raised as a finding: the h2-to-h3 ratio at 375** is 24.03px against 20px, a ratio of 1.2 (it is 1.27 at 1440). That is thin, but in `shots/home-375-t01-y812.png` the 64px of space above each h2 carries the distinction and the hierarchy reads correctly. Raising the h2 clamp minimum to `1.625rem` would give 26px and a 1.3 ratio. I am not asking for it: the checks pass and changing type scale after a passing render is exactly the "keep changing direction without new evidence" failure mode.
- **Considered and not raised: section intro sentences are inconsistent.** Selected work, Writing and Contact each have a one-line intro under the h2; What I'm building and About go straight into content. Invisible at the gestalt, and it is a copy pattern rather than a layout one. Mentioned here for the writer's awareness only.
- **Optional, for the writer:** the article meta row currently renders a single brown word because no article has a publication date. It reads fine as a category label. When real dates land, re-check the two-item flex row at 375, where `September 12, 2026` plus a 24px gap plus a category may wrap.

## Proposed decision-log update

**Q2 or Q5, depending on where the coordinator files structural choices. Actual choice:** the site uses two annotation colors with distinct, exhaustive roles rather than one. Brown `#6B4A2F` carries classification and dating (company deck, writing category, article meta, article list markers). `--ink-2` `#4D4D4D` carries non-classifying secondary text (Selected Work source line, footer line, article blockquote). **Rejected alternative:** a single brown annotation color for both, which would have been simpler but would have flattened the difference between a descriptor that completes a heading and an attribution the reader can skip. **Evidence:** rendered comparison at 3x, `crop-brown.png` against `crop-grey.png`; both pairs pass contrast on white (7.94:1 and 8.45:1). **Given up:** one less rule to remember, and a slightly less rigid "one accent, one job" story.

**Q4 (verification), candidate.** The method-line measurement is genuine Q4 material and it is a check that actually failed. Asserting a computed `font-size` is not enough for SVG text: `getComputedStyle` reports the *specified* 15px at every width, which passes, while the *rendered* size is 13.88px at 640px because the viewBox scales. The only check that catches it multiplies the specified size by `getBoundingClientRect().width / viewBox.baseVal.width`. It failed, and it would have shipped invisibly otherwise, because nothing about the page looks broken.

**No new Q3 note from this round.** Nothing in D02 involved Evan rejecting or correcting an agent.
