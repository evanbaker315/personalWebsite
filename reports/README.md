# Agent reports

Every worker and reviewer writes a report using `TEMPLATE.md`, including on partial or blocked work. Use `reports/<task-id>-<role>-<round>.md`, for example `W02-writer-01.md` or `R01-investor-01.md`. The coordinator supplies the exact unique path with the task assignment.

Reports contain work and ideas, not private raw context. These files may be committed to a public repository. No private background, credentials, employer internals, or speculative allegations. Reference a private claim record by identifier without copying its contents.

Keep screenshots, fetches, and supporting local review evidence outside `verification/` until preparing the assignment's exact three artifacts. Link actual files and identify the candidate revision. Empty templates are not evidence. Do not invent reports for tasks that have not run.

The coordinator also owns `candidate.md` (created when a candidate exists) and `review-disposition.md` (created when audience findings exist). Review disposition maps each finding to accepted/rejected/deferred/needs Evan, reason, task owner, follow-up task, and resolution evidence. Each reviewer sees the candidate independently before reading other reports.

Reports do not replace `DECISIONS.md`. Propose concise decision updates for the coordinator to integrate; Evan writes final Q3 himself.
